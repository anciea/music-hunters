  <what-built>Complete audio playback stack: AudioHandler with audio_service, full player screen (DraggableScrollableSheet), enhanced mini player with progress line and tap-to-expand, seek bar with live drag timestamp, play/pause/skip/shuffle/repeat controls, background audio with notification bar controls, queue management with reorder/remove, and long-press context menu on search results.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Run the app on an Android device or emulator:

    1. **Search and play (PLAY-01):** Search for a song, tap a result. Audio should start playing. Mini player bar appears.

    2. **Mini player (MINI-01, MINI-02, MINI-03):** Verify mini player shows at bottom of all 4 tabs with track title, artist, play/pause, and 2dp green progress line. Tap mini player — full player sheet should slide up.

    3. **Full player controls (PLAY-02, PLAY-03, PLAY-04, PLAY-05):**
       - Seek bar: drag thumb and verify live timestamp label appears above it. Release to seek.
       - Play/pause: tap to toggle. Verify icon changes.
       - Shuffle: tap to toggle. Verify icon turns green when on.
       - Repeat: tap to cycle off -> one -> all. Verify icon changes (repeat, repeat_one with green).

    4. **Queue management (QUE-01, QUE-02, QUE-03, QUE-04):**
       - Go back to search. Long-press a result — context menu should show "Play Now", "Play Next", "Add to Queue".
       - Tap "Add to Queue" on 2-3 tracks.
       - Open full player, tap queue icon. Queue sheet shows all tracks.
       - Drag a track to reorder. Verify order changes.
       - Tap X to remove a track. Verify it disappears.
       - Tap skip next/previous to navigate queue.

    5. **Background audio (PLAY-06, PLAY-07, PLAY-08):**
       - While music plays, press the home button. Audio should continue.
       - Pull down notification bar — should show album art, track title, and play/pause + skip controls.
       - Tap pause in notification — music pauses. Tap play — music resumes.
       - Tap skip next in notification — next track plays (if queue has items).
       - If headset connected: press headset button to play/pause.

    6. **Audio focus (PLAY-09):**
       - While music plays, have another app play audio briefly. Music should pause or duck.
       - Receive a phone call (or simulate) — music should pause.
